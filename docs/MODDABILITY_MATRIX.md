The stable release combines the clean TASM panels/black lenses, white alternate,
menu readability, yellow mission marker, free Snap Sweep and purchased Rebound.
Nine Glob assets and two ARM9 data words differ from the original. Every build
rereads all 3,733 resources and verifies the known profile. The full suite passes
76 tests; runtime acceptance and limits are recorded in TASM1_REBOUND.md.
ROMs, overlays, extracted assets, RAM, captures and saves remain private.
