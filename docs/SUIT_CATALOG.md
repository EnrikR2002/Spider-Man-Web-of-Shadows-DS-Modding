# Suit concept catalog

66 directions, 2026-10-04. These are proposed mod mechanics, not implemented abilities or canonical costume powers.

Includes alternate Spider-people and explicitly inspired designs. Faithful character bodies, clothing, props, and extra limbs need additional model/animation work. See [the research plan](NEW_SUIT_DESIGN.md) for the third-suit requirements and two detailed kits.
